Bed Remote

  • Coming soon to Google Play
  • Android

TNTech TV Remote

  • Coming soon to Google Play
  • Android
  • Google TV

Turns your Android phone into a fast remote for Google TV and Android TV. It pairs with the on-screen code, like Google's own remote. Nothing to install on the TV.

  • Finds TVs on your Wi-Fi automatically
  • D-pad or touchpad, voice search and keyboard
  • Encrypted, phone-to-TV only, ~1.4 MB

QNetd Add-on

  • Open source
  • Home Assistant
  • Proxmox

A Home Assistant add-on that runs a Corosync QNetd tie-breaker, so a two-node Proxmox VE cluster keeps quorum when one node goes down.

  • Adds the third vote a two-node cluster needs
  • Runs on hardware you already have
  • Includes a QDevice setup script

Spec sheet

Private by default

No accounts, ads or analytics. Your data stays on your device and goes only to the hardware you control.

02

Local first

Apps talk straight to your bed, TV or server over Bluetooth or your home network. No cloud in the middle.

03

Small and fast

Native code, tiny downloads and no background battery drain. They open fast and just work.
